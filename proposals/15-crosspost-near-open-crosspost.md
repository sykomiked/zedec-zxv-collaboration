## What you are building

Write once, publish to X and Farcaster from one place — a multi-platform editor,
scheduler and campaign board on NEAR.

## Why we are a good user for you

We have **15 published entries on NEAR Builders** and no distribution at all.
Fifteen posts describing a deployed system, seen by whoever happened to browse.

That is exactly the problem your product solves, and we are an unusually
documented case: 15 pieces of technical content with real figures, ready to
publish, sitting behind a tool we do not have.

## How it could fit

**Option A — we use Crosspost to publish the corpus.** Fifteen entries, one
editor, scheduled across X and Farcaster.

**Option B — a case study.** "This project had 15 technical posts and zero reach"
is a concrete before/after for your own marketing.

**Option C — `social.near` interop.** We already publish to `social.near`; if
Crosspost reads or writes there, we can share the shapes and the quirks.

## Why this is worth your time

- **A ready-made content corpus.** Fifteen posts, all factual, all with numbers.
- **A real distribution gap to close.** We are the target user, not a
  hypothetical one.
- **We will report the result.** If Crosspost moves our reach, we will say so in
  a repository that already carries our whole record.

## What we are asking

Access, or a walkthrough. If it works we will publish the corpus through it and
tell people.
